1. Overview

Free GST Billing Software is a free, full-featured, offline-native GST billing and taxation desktop application built for Indian SMEs, traders, freelancers, and service providers.

Local-First Guarantee: Your invoices, client records, bank details, and ledgers remain 100% on your own computer. The software never requires sign-in, charges zero subscription fees, and runs completely offline.

Core Capabilities:

  • Document Generation: Tax Invoices, Quotations/Estimates, Proforma Invoices, Credit Notes, Bills of Supply, and Delivery Challans.
  • Accurate Taxation: Intrastate (CGST + SGST), Interstate (IGST), Union Territory (UTGST), Cess, Reverse Charge (RCM), and Section 194Q / 206C TDS/TCS.
  • Document Customization: Professional A4/Letter PDF templates and 58mm/80mm thermal receipt printing.
  • Statutory Compliance: One-click GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B JSON exports for direct upload to the GST Portal, and GSTR-2B ITC reconciliation.
  • Seamless Payments: Dynamic UPI payment QR codes embedded directly onto invoices.

2. Quick Start

Running the Desktop Application

  1. macOS: Download and open the .dmg installer (Apple Silicon arm64), drag the application to your Applications folder, and launch.
  2. Windows: Run Free GST Billing Software Setup 1.10.81.exe. The installer configures background services, creates desktop shortcuts, and launches the app.
  3. Web / Development Mode: Run npm run dev from the terminal, then visit http://localhost:47371 in any modern browser.

3. Business Setup

On first run, the welcome wizard prompts for your essential business details:

  • Business Name & Trade Name: Appears prominently in the header of all invoices.
  • GSTIN: Your 15-digit Goods & Services Tax Identification Number. The first 2 digits determine your State Code for automated CGST/SGST vs IGST calculation.
  • Address, Email & Phone: Listed in the supplier section.
  • Bank & UPI Details: Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC, and UPI ID (e.g. merchant@upi). Generating an invoice automatically renders a scannable payment QR code.
  • Multiple Businesses: Manage up to 10 distinct business profiles from Settings → Business Profiles.

4. Invoicing & Billing

Click New Invoice (or press Ctrl+N / Cmd+N):

  1. Select Client: Choose an existing client or enter a new client's name and GSTIN. If their state differs from your business state, the engine automatically selects IGST.
  2. Add Items: Type the product or service description, HSN/SAC code, quantity, unit (e.g., PCS, KGS, BOX, MTR), and rate.
  3. Tax Rate: Select 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, or custom exempt rates.
  4. Discounts: Add percentage or flat discounts per line item, or a whole-invoice discount.
  5. Preview & Save: Click Save & View PDF or Print. Invoices are stored in the local SQLite/JSON ledger.

5. GST Tax Engine

The mathematical computation engine is rigorously verified with 356 automated tests covering Indian tax rules:

  • Intrastate Supplies: Automatically splits tax into equal CGST and SGST halves.
  • Union Territories: For supplies within UTs without legislature (Chandigarh, Andaman & Nicobar, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Dadra & Nagar Haveli), tax is split into CGST and UTGST.
  • Interstate Supplies: Applies single IGST rate.
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): Embedded tax is backed out if tax-inclusive, marking tax liability on recipient.
  • TCS & TDS: Section 206C(1H) / 194Q threshold logic applied accurately over cumulative ₹50 Lakh financial year purchases.

6. Print & PDF Settings

Under Settings → Print & PDF Settings, customize your document appearance:

  • Templates: Aurora (Modern Slate/Blue), Editorial (Classic serif), Executive (Corporate Navy), Whisper (Minimalist hairline), and more.
  • Thermal Printing: Direct raw or graphical printing for 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS receipt printers.
  • Header & Letterhead: Upload custom letterheads (PNG/JPG) with automatic invoice header suppression.
  • Signatures & Stamps: Upload authorized signatory signature image for automatic placement above the signature line.

7. Reprint Tracking

Reprint Badge Feature: When Show REPRINT badge on reprints is enabled, reprinting any previously printed invoice displays a distinct REPRINT · Copy #N indicator at the top of the invoice, preventing duplicate billing confusion.

Configure this in Settings → Print & PDF Settings → Reprint tracking (recommended for retail and POS businesses).

8. Inventory & Stock

Under Products, monitor your catalog:

  • Stock Movements: Invoices decrease available stock; Purchase bills increase stock.
  • Low Stock Warnings: Set minimum stock thresholds to trigger dashboard alert badges.
  • Bulk CSV Import/Export: Import existing product catalogs from Excel or Tally CSV files.

9. GST Returns & ITC Reconciliation

Under GST Returns:

  • GSTR-1 JSON Export: Automatically compiles outward supplies into the government portal schema. Download the JSON file and upload directly to gst.gov.in.
  • GSTR-3B Summary: Summarizes eligible ITC, tax payable, and reverse charge liabilities for monthly filing.
  • GSTR-2B Matching: Upload your official GSTR-2B JSON or Excel file from the portal to reconcile claimed Input Tax Credit with supplier filings.

10. Backup & Privacy

Your data is stored in the _system/data/ directory. To create a backup:

  • Click Settings → Backup & Restore → Download Full Backup to save an encrypted ZIP archive.
  • Google Drive Sync: Optionally connect your Google Drive to automate off-site backup copies.
  • PIN Lock: Set an application PIN under Settings → Security to protect sensitive billing data with Scrypt hashing and transient session tokens.
